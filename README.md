The dashboard is designed to provide a clear and interactive view of Vrinda Store's performance, using slicers for Month, Category, and Channel so users can filter and customize the view easily.

Orders vs Sales

In the top-left section, I have shown Orders vs Sales over the selected months (May to June).

The blue bars represent the total Sales Amount, and the orange line represents the Number of Orders.

We notice that October had the highest sales peak, indicating strong performance during festive seasons.

Sales: Men vs Women

On the top center, there is a pie chart breaking down sales between Men (48%) and Women (52%).

Women slightly lead in purchases, helping us strategize marketing campaigns toward female customers.

Order Status

Moving right, we have an Order Status pie chart.

67% of the orders were successfully delivered, 25% got cancelled, and 8% were refunded.

This highlights a good delivery success rate but also indicates opportunities to reduce cancellations.

Sales: Top Five States

Below, there’s a horizontal bar chart showing the Top 5 States by sales volume.

Bengaluru leads with 678 orders, followed closely by Mysuru and Kalyan.

This helps identify where our key customer bases are located.

Orders: Age vs Gender

In the middle bottom section, the Orders by Age and Gender chart shows:

Adults contribute the most, especially men (33.33%) and women (16.67%).

Teenagers and Seniors have lower engagement, indicating a potential growth area.

Orders: Channels

Finally, the Orders Channels pie chart shows that all orders in this filtered view are through Flipkart (100%).

This highlights Flipkart as a key partner platform during this time period.

Conclusion: "In summary, this dashboard gives quick insights into customer demographics, order performance, and channel efficiency. It also reveals areas of success, like strong adult engagement and city-wise dominance, and areas of improvement, like minimizing order cancellations."
